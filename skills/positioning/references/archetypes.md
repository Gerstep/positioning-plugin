# Archetype picker (90 seconds)

Products get copied overnight; the logical argument for a brand keeps
shrinking. Feel is the durable differentiator. The archetype gives the
position a feel — one archetype, not three.

## Step 1 — the core promise

Ask: "At its core, what does your brand promise people?" Four options:

| Quadrant | The promise | Archetypes |
|---|---|---|
| **Structure** | safety, order, things under control | Caregiver · Ruler · Creator |
| **Connection** | belonging, joy, being understood | Everyman · Lover · Jester |
| **Mastery** | change, power, the impossible done | Hero · Outlaw · Magician |
| **Freedom** | understanding, honesty, open horizons | Innocent · Sage · Explorer |

## Step 2 — pick one of three

**Structure**

- **Caregiver** — "you're protected." Voice: warm, reassuring. (Anthropic
  wears this in AI: safety, safety, safety.)
- **Ruler** — "we set the standard." Voice: authoritative, premium.
- **Creator** — "build something that lasts." Voice: imaginative,
  craft-proud. (OpenAI's creative arc — "reimagined with ChatGPT".)

**Connection**

- **Everyman** — "this is for people like us." Voice: plain, unpretentious.
- **Lover** — "you'll love this." Voice: warm, sensory. (Lovable — it's in
  the name.)
- **Jester** — "don't take it all so seriously." Voice: playful, quick.

**Mastery**

- **Hero** — "rise to it." Voice: bold, direct, motivating.
- **Outlaw** — "break the old way." Voice: defiant, unfiltered. (Robinhood,
  in a regulated industry — note the price below.)
- **Magician** — "watch this become real." Voice: visionary, a touch of
  wonder. (Cursor announcing a funding round as "Series A and magic".)

**Freedom**

- **Innocent** — "it's simple and good." Voice: clear, optimistic.
- **Sage** — "we understand it deepest." Voice: precise, evidence-first.
- **Explorer** — "go further." Voice: independent, restless.

## Step 3 — one voice line

Write one sentence for the pack: "Launch copy should sound [voice traits],
never [the opposite]." Every headline, banner, and announcement should
reinforce the archetype — the felt promise and the stated position must point
the same way.

## Notes

- **Shadow check.** Each archetype has a failure mode: Lover turns creepy,
  Ruler turns tyrant, Outlaw turns reckless, Magician turns snake oil,
  Caregiver turns smothering. Add one "never be" line to guard it.
- **Regulated industries.** The wheel still applies; the dominant emotional
  trigger is usually trust. Outlaw is possible — Robinhood proved it — but
  you pay for it with the regulator. Pick deliberately, not accidentally.
- **Every word is a tell.** "Reimagined" is Creator; "magic" is Magician;
  "safe" is Caregiver. Check the mind virus against the archetype — they must
  rhyme.
